Numbers appear after you validate enough sample decisions. Your decision never erases the original Exculpata label.

What these numbers mean
Precision
Of the sources Exculpata included, the share your validation also included.
Recall
Of all validated sources that should be included, the share Exculpata found.
Elusion
The share of sources labeled “Not identified” that your validation says should have been included.
Richness
The share of the validated sample that actually meets the criterion.
Error
The overall share of validated labels that disagree with your decision.
