# Exculpata Trademark Policy

> **Not legal advice.**

The Apache-2.0 [license](LICENSE) covers Exculpata's code. It does **not**
grant rights to the Exculpata name, logo, or other brand assets (the "Marks").
Apache-2.0 section 6 says the same.

## Allowed without asking

- Factual references, such as "based on Exculpata" or "compatible with
  Exculpata".
- Redistributing unmodified official releases under the Exculpata name.
- Community use: talks, articles, tutorials, meetups, and links to the project.

## Not allowed

- Using the Marks for a modified version or fork. Forks must pick a new name
  and remove the Exculpata logo and branding.
- Implying your product, service, or organization is official, endorsed by, or
  affiliated with Exculpata when it is not.
- Using the Marks in your own product names, company names, or domain names.

For anything else, open an issue and ask first.
